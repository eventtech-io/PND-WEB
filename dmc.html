World DJ Championships Production Programme

PND and Event Tech Research are developing a broadcast-capable technical production architecture for the DMC World DJ Championships 2026 Camden programme.

The project spans multiple venues including The Dark Horse, The Stables, KOKO and Dingwalls, focusing on resilient audio systems, rapid booth deployment, multichannel recording, live capture and stream-ready infrastructure.

Built around a broadcast-production philosophy designed to support: consistent competitor conditions, protected signal paths, redundant recording systems and scalable live delivery.

Core Systems

• Triple-position booth workflow
• EtherCON / Cat infrastructure
• UPS-backed DJ systems
• Broadcast-ready audio splits
• Multi-camera capture
• Stream redundancy planning
• Live archive recording
