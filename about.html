About

PND means Party Never Dies

Party Never Dies is a name that carries contradiction. It speaks to something ephemeral — a moment, a gathering, a signal in the dark — treated with permanence. The party as metaphor for live culture. The refusal to die as commitment to documenting, building and preserving what happens in those spaces.

What we are

PND exists as a creative and technical collective. We are artists, engineers, filmmakers, designers, coders, sound people and visual creators who work together on projects that sit at the intersection of culture and technology. We build systems, create installations, document events, experiment with signals and develop tools for live experience.

What we connect

The collective links underground culture, live events, digital art, audio, video, software and systems design. We work with venues, festivals, artists, labels and cultural institutions. Our projects range from one-night installations to ongoing research programmes.

Technical backbone

Event Tech Research provides technical research, engineering and infrastructure support to the collective. They develop the systems, tools and technical frameworks that enable PND projects to exist. This partnership allows the creative and technical sides to develop in parallel.
