Ed

Cinematographer / Lecturer / Documentary / Corporate Film

Ed is an award-winning cinematographer and university lecturer at The University of Staffordshire. His work spans documentary, corporate production, commercial content and broadcast-ready delivery across the UK, Europe and worldwide.

Film practice

Experienced with most digital and film formats, Ed works primarily across documentary and corporate productions, bringing a practical cinematographer’s eye to live culture, people, place and story.

Screenings & delivery

His films have screened in the UK and North America, with delivery experience across Netflix, Amazon and iTunes workflows.

Training & education

Alongside lecturing, Ed has attended a BFI short filmmaking course at the National Film and Television School.

Camera package

Ed owns a full Sony FX9 kit and is equipped for most corporate shoots, documentary work, branded content and location-led production.

Selected client experience

Nando's, The Premier League, The Discovery Channel, Marks and Spencer, Dixons Carphone, Bensons for Beds, Sony Records, Columbia Records, Churchill, Steelite, Wedgwood, FatFace, NHS and more.
