PND Founder

Chris Mansouri

Technical Director / Lighting / Audio / Video / Digital Systems

Chris Mansouri is a British technical professional, director of Habitual Limited and co-founder of PND. His work sits across live events, broadcast production, lighting, audio, video and digital systems.

PND role

Chris brings practical production knowledge, technical direction and cross-discipline event experience to the collective.

Habitual Limited

Habitual Limited operates in information technology service activities, supporting Chris’s wider work across technical production and digital systems.

Technical field

Chris is connected with engineering practice across lighting, audio and video, with a focus on the working realities of live and broadcast environments.

Collective direction

Within PND, Chris helps bridge technical execution with creative production, supporting the collective’s live culture and art-tech ambitions.
