PND Founder

Rob Handyside

Event Technology / Audio / RF / Networks / Research

Rob Handyside is an audio and event-technology professional, founder of Event Tech Research and co-founder of PND. His work connects live sound, RF, networking, software, systems design and field-tested research for cultural production.

PND role

Rob provides technical research, engineering direction and infrastructure thinking for the collective, helping convert creative concepts into stable live systems.

Event Tech Research

Event Tech Research acts as the technical and research backbone connected to PND, supporting work across live-event infrastructure, software-defined monitoring, audio systems, RF, networking and production workflows.

Background

Rob has a senior live-sound and event-technology background, including large-scale event production, technical evaluation, system design and broadcast-style audio workflows.

Practice

His current work links R&D, live events, performing arts support, installation design, training and practical field engineering.
